Mathematical and Physical Research Group

Focusing on the theoretical discourse and dilemma, paradox identification, mathematical utilization on physical systems, and the theoretical-practical gap in principled physical theories.

Welcome to the RHINE-MAPR - Mathematical and Physical Research Group, a laboratory section of RHINELAB focuses on researching the foundation, the inherent details and non-trivial ‘just a theory, quantum mechanics is there and just use it’ (for example, obviously), and the re-concretization of theoretical and practical knowledge, in a coherent and from scratch altitude 1.

Working and principles

For now, let us introduce briefly of the details concerning the members of RHINELAB Physics Group Members, and what we are to expect people working inside this fundamental angle of research. Within this group we investigate and research problems in the field of Physics, in general. The hierarchy of the group will be as follows, starting as a branching office from RHINELAB in full. \[ \text{RHINELAB Physics Group}\rightarrow\left\{\begin{matrix} \text{Mathematics and Theoretical Physics Subgroup}\\ \text{Nuclear Physics Subgroup} \\ \text{Mathematics and Foundation Building Subgroup} \end{matrix}\right. \] And, subsequently, for RHINELAB Physics Group, such includes some of the major research anecdotes that are of focus for foundation of physical knowledge as we realize it so: \[ \text{RHINELAB Physics Group}\rightarrow \left\{ \begin{matrix} \text{Mathematical Physics}\\ \text{Statistics and Probability Theory} \\ \text{Quantum Physics} \\ \text{Theoretical Nuclear Physics} \end{matrix} \right. \]

The focus, and the contents in specificity of focus can be and will be different later on, however within the scope of foundational build-up of present, the plan is to rely on those fields to establish a sound foundation before attempting any serious works.
